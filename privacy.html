Privacy Policy

AutoPost AI is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data. We do not sell your data — ever.

1. Data We Collect

Data TypePurposeRetention

We do not collect names, email addresses, passwords, payment information, or any biometric data.

2. How We Use Your Data

Data collected is used exclusively to:

  • Authenticate your TikTok account via OAuth 2.0
  • Initialize and complete video uploads to TikTok's API
  • Display your authorization status within the App session
No data is shared with third parties, advertisers, analytics providers, or any external service beyond TikTok's own API endpoints.

3. OAuth & TikTok API

When you connect your TikTok account, you are redirected to TikTok's official OAuth flow. AutoPost AI never sees your TikTok password. The access token returned is held only in your active server session and is immediately discarded when the session ends or you log out.

TikTok's own Privacy Policy governs how TikTok processes data on their end: tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy

4. Cookies & Local Storage

The App uses only a single server-side session cookie to maintain your authenticated state during a session. No tracking cookies, third-party cookies, or persistent identifiers are used.

5. Data Security

All communications between the App and TikTok's API are conducted over HTTPS/TLS. Access tokens are stored in memory only and never written to disk, databases, or logs. Video files are deleted from temporary storage immediately after a successful upload response is received.

6. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Regardless of your location, you have the right to:

  • Know what data is held about you (there is none beyond your active session)
  • Revoke App access at any time via TikTok → Settings → Privacy → Apps & Websites
  • Request deletion of any data — contact us and we will confirm no data is stored

7. Children's Privacy

AutoPost AI is not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has used the App, contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The "Last Updated" date will always reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the App after updates constitutes acceptance.
